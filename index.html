Oral Surgery Assess

Chairside decision-support tools for oral surgery and dental sedation.

Developed by Aiden McCann · Dentist with special interest in oral surgery

A set of four tools for use at the chairside. Each works through a structured assessment and produces a summary you can act on. They support clinical judgement and do not replace it, a full history and examination, or the consent process.

  • Third Molar Assessment Structured assessment for extraction, giving a surgical difficulty score, clinical findings, and the recommended level of clinician.
  • Sedation Pre-Assessment Full pre-sedation record: patient details, physical status, medical history, airway, medications, MDAS and IOSN scores, and discharge arrangements.
  • Local Anaesthetic Maximum Dose Calculates safe maximum doses by agent and body weight, with cartridge counts.
  • ASA Assessment Guided ASA physical status grading for conscious sedation, mapping the confirmed grade to the appropriate sedation setting.

None of the tools store patient identifiers, and nothing you enter leaves your device.
