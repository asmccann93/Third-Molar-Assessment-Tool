Oral Surgery · Chairside support tools

Assessment Tools

Developed by Aiden McCann

Chairside decision-support for oral surgery. Each tool works through an assessment in a structured order and produces a summary you can print or save to the patient's records. Both work offline once added to your home screen.

Third Molar Assessment Assess upper and lower third molars when extraction is being considered. Indicates anticipated surgical difficulty and the appropriate level of clinician. Sedation Pre-Assessment Structured pre-sedation record for adults aged 18 and over, with automatic BMI, MDAS and IOSN scoring and a signed, printable summary.
These are chairside decision-support tools. They do not replace clinical judgement, a full medical history, or the assessing clinician's responsibility for the care of the patient.
