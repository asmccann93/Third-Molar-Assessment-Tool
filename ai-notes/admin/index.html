Add someone

Initials are 2–4 letters and are how notes, the implant tool’s access list and transcription jobs know who someone is, so they cannot be changed later. Adding someone gives you a setup link for them.

Every change here is one write to the account store. The free plan includes only 100 writes a month for the whole store (going over blocks sign-in for 30 days) and this page cannot see how many are left, so make changes deliberately; it allows 5 an hour and 10 a day. Changes can take up to about 40 seconds to reach every server: a disabled colleague is signed out within that time. Disabling, deleting or demoting an admin takes about 10 seconds, while the page checks another admin is still left.
